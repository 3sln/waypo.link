# Free service retention grace

This is a Waypo.link implementation policy, separate from the open identity/service protocols and storage quota. Initial free hosted storage is 500,000,000 bytes. A free account with at least 100,000,000 current or previously recorded peak bytes, or 14 distinct days of explicit human activity, qualifies for the additional month of inactivity grace. The 100MB threshold lets a free account earn storage-based grace before filling its quota; it is not a new storage allowance, pricing rule, or signal that public launch economics are approved.

During the first year, a two-month gap without explicit human activity triggers contact. Ordinary grace is one month after an accepted opening notice; qualifying substantial use adds one month. The existing verified-contact, notice-delivery, shared/paid/protected-resource safeguards and explicit retention execution controls still apply. Background sync and token refresh are not human activity. This threshold change does not enable deletion or public signup.

The portable engine remains configurable; the 100MB override applies only to the free hosted preset. Deferred legacy paid policy behavior is unchanged. Engine scenarios compare 99,999,999 with 100,000,000 bytes and 13 with 14 human days against actual calendar-month deadlines.
