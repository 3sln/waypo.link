# Prepaid outbound attempts

> Source-development reference: open-source Waypo `5e9b55b` in this isolated candidate. Public operation remains disabled; work units are not a financial guarantee.

This is provider implementation policy, independent of OIDC capabilities, subscription plans and app permissions. Optional app pushes and device-verification challenges must reserve installation ordinary credits before their persisted delivery claims are committed. The local trusted `reserveOutboundInTransaction(tx)` port accepts only the current metadata transaction; no public request selects its cost, category or exemption. Portable self-hosted engines may omit this policy. The application mounts separate optional and provider-notice callbacks whenever its installation period policy is configured. Hosted mandatory work-policy validation supplies that policy even for identity-only profiles. Runtime qualification remains separate from this source integration.

The initial provider-selected weight is the explicitly validated `notifications-write` work weight for **each destination and external attempt**, including retries. One message sent to seven devices consumes seven attempts; a digest delivered once to one destination consumes one attempt. The enqueue work charge is separate from transport work. Work units are not dollars, and this weight is not a financial safety guarantee.

The callback and attempt/lease persistence share the same transaction. Commit failure rolls back both and makes no transport call. Successful reservation followed by process loss is spent, with no refund. Existing claim fencing, authority checks, per-route retry ceilings and Web Push transport deadlines remain necessary. A lease is clamped to the prepaid period boundary; remote acceptance after a lost response remains ambiguous, so this does not promise exactly-once delivery.

A closed period-budget refusal defers the item without incrementing transport attempts, creating a claim or invoking transport. Full daily/monthly retry deadlines are retained durably; device-verification work instead expires at its existing ten-minute deadline. Nonretryable operator-state problems retain pending work and use a five-minute readiness recheck, never an unbounded rapid retry. Unknown errors propagate and roll back. Notification expiry and independent digest wakeups must remain scheduled even when outbound delivery is deferred.

Provider retention/account notices use a separate trusted `reserveProviderNoticeInTransaction` essential reservation seam after verifying the target device, selected only by the provider lifecycle workflow. Generic app pushes never receive that reserve. A bounded trusted `{status:"deferred",retryAt}` no-send result passes through maintenance and both identity/funding job schedulers. A matching live claim restores only its own pre-dispatch attempt count; prior actual/uncertain attempts are retained even at the saturated count. Deferral never establishes accepted/rejected notice delivery or starts the response/deletion grace window. Contact-outage protection covers the deferred deadline plus a day, and accepted contact delivery retains existing hold-clearing behavior. Invalid results remain outages. Account/device churn admission, all other transports and raw incoming Worker cost remain outside this component. Prepayment is conservative: missing keys, crashes or failures after reservation do not refund even if no packet ultimately leaves the transport.
