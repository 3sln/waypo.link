# Forgotten paid renewal and recoverable personal storage

Implementation contract proposed 2026-11-19, for coordinator review before implementation. This is provider implementation and Waypo.link policy, not a mandatory identity/discovery extension. No billing, retention execution or vendor calls are activated by this document. The currently free-only launch does not erase this unfinished requirement.

## User and service flow

A paid user who stops interacting first receives the existing inactivity notice and reminders at opening, day7 and day21 through verified contacts. Keep the existing three-calendar-month paid response window, and longer substantial-history/contact-outage safeguards. Background sync, renewals and automated app traffic are not human responses. The account page explains the exact response date, funded-through date, cancellation status and eventual archive deadline. A genuine authenticated check-in with Turnstile stops an undispatched abandonment episode; it never silently re-enables externally canceled renewal.

After an unanswered response deadline, schedule cancellation of **future renewal**, never an immediate refund/revocation of an already funded term. Continue normal authorized paid storage through the verified paid-through/trial boundary, unless a separate abuse/security decision applies. Freeze/archive no earlier than the later of response deadline and that boundary. The current entitlement function pauses optional writes whenever an inactivity episode exists: that is a concrete behavior to reconcile with this funded-term contract, not a reason to shorten the purchased term.

The account then offers an immutable dated archive with “Download,” “Restore” and its exact deletion date. Sign-in, contact repair, support/appeal and export remain accessible under their own bounded owner controls. Downloads and check-ins do not extend the archive deadline. Restoration is explicit, validates current capacity and grants no new renewal agreement. A new payment alone does not restore archived contents or grant an app access.

Distinguish two cases. Abandonment of the local identity may eventually delete that identity and its personal archive after notices and safeguards, as requested. Funding lapse for an otherwise active user reclaims optional personal resources only; their free identity remains. Shared/separately paid resources, independent service principals and other providers' data are excluded in both cases. Unresolved real ownership/payment dependencies require transfer or staff resolution, not silent destruction.

## Archive representation: freeze and retain, do not copy entire accounts

Prefer a frozen immutable personal storage generation with a paged manifest and service-owned retention references to already immutable objects. Do not create a second full 10GB copy merely to call it an archive, and do not keep a live actor/cache or open owner export lease for months. Existing physical liability remains charged until confirmed retirement. Metadata pages and any new cold log segments reserve their own additional bounded bytes/rows before writes.

A manifest contains only the owner/installation binding, archive ID, lifecycle revision, policy/funding decision, immutable retention deadline, schema revision, paged log descriptors (app namespace, log identity/incarnation, retained floor/head and entry/segment integrity data), and blob key/version/size/hash/encoding/backend references. It is private, integrity checked and anchored by a manifest digest. Include the cursor-signing/incarnation state needed to preserve historical cursor meaning; do not export signing secrets in the user's download. Preserve namespace isolation and deletion/trim floors. Native objects stay provider-at-rest encrypted under the chosen storage model; this is not E2EE.

The old policy adapter's `encrypted:true` confirmation contract assumed an encrypted legacy archive object. Define a reviewed protection proof for the new manifest/object model instead of returning that boolean without implementation. If an encrypted private manifest is retained, use a persisted versioned key binding and include its liability/restore support; never silently encrypt native payloads into a format that breaks direct read URLs.

## Durable state and cross-store boundaries

Use a bounded durable operation record keyed by archiveId, with exact subject, scope, decision/resource revision, lifecycle revision, configuration bindings, cancellation intent and original retention policy snapshot. States are `preparing -> frozen -> capturing -> ready -> restoring -> restored` or `expiring -> purged`; failures remain resumable/quarantined, never implicit success. Each phase has a bounded cursor and compare-and-swap revision. At most one archive and one restore operation per account; retries reuse their operation IDs.

Control first validates current policy/contact/dependencies/funding and records the prepare intent. Account acceptance atomically fences writes, uploads/publication, trim and new signaling/storage leases against that exact lifecycle revision. Active read/export pins drain within their original finite deadlines. It then captures bounded pages from a now-frozen state. Every asynchronous object operation rechecks the current accepted revision before publication. A later human response/new funding before acceptance cancels the intent; after acceptance it requires explicit abort/restore reconciliation. Do not claim a single transaction across control SQL, account SQL, Native identity and object storage.

Control finalizes archived policy only after the account returns an idempotent ready receipt with exact manifest digest, retained byte/row liability, immutable expiry and verified capture. Missing/changed state fails closed. A late/unknown ready response is reconciled by reading that exact operation, not by starting a fresh archive. Capture progress cannot authorize terminal identity deletion or grant management.

Private maintenance context must name the exact archive operation, owner, route and lifecycle/configuration revisions, with a short physical proof lifetime and current checks. It must not accept an arbitrary app/owner JSON selector or reuse expired prepaid credit. Essential bounded preservation/expiry work and optional compaction use the same global wallet with their proper lane; an archive job is not permission to drain a whole account in one receipt.

## Capture, download and restore bounds

Reuse current page/stream bounds: at most eight log captures per durable step; log reads at most64entries/256KiB with at most four cold segments/1MiB bounded verification per page; blob metadata pages at the existing fixed page ceiling; native object reads streamed/version pinned with the existing maximum range and finite120-second physical request lifetime. No whole-file buffering, unbounded object list or whole-account SQL transaction.

Archive download requires a current Native owner session, expected-user fence and short SID-bound download lease. It must verify the archive binding/deadline both before and after I/O. Leases are capped by immutable expiry, with finite stock and explicit release/automatic cleanup. Offer a paged manifest plus resumable exact-version content; a browser/CLI may build a streaming bundle. Download links cannot become write URLs, app bearer authority, public archive paths or indefinite presigned archive exports.

Restore uses the existing explicit challenge/Turnstile, current session and bounded one-hour restore permit, clipped by archive expiry. Admit current logical/physical/row capacity and work **before** a restore begins. Reuse retained immutable objects where possible; stage any new metadata under the restore operation, with no partial visibility. Rebuild/verify bounded pages and the manifest digest, then atomically publish the account lifecycle as active. Control finalization is idempotent. Preserve log incarnation/cursor history when that history is retained; a trimmed cursor still errors. Old app grants are revalidated; restoration neither resurrects revoked grants nor old expired leases. Session/account switching or permit expiry stops further work and retains resumable staged liability without publication.

## Tenure, expiration and uncertainty

Keep the implemented policy: `min(180, 30 + 30 * floor(completedPaidMonths / 12))` days. Count only completed verified earned paid periods; future annual prepayment is not instantly twelve months of tenure. Snapshot the earned duration and immutable expiry when the archive is committed. Repeated downloads, reminder failures, retries, restore initiation or plan switches do not roll the deadline forward. An operator incident hold is explicit, bounded/audited and charged; no hidden forever-paid immunity.

No new downloads/restores start at expiry. Existing physical reads cannot extend their originally clipped pin/deadline. A restore racing expiry needs one accepted durable winner: a committed restore owns the retained objects; otherwise expiry tombstones the archive and staged restore is cleaned up. Confirmed expiry schedules bounded manifest/log/blob/native retirement and then policy/identity finalization appropriate to scope. Do not run terminal whole-account purge for a resource-only funding lapse.

Unknown object writes/deletes, cancellation calls, manifest publication or restore publication retain their reserved bytes/rows and operation records. They do not refund quota, return fleet slices or claim deletion. Reconcile exact object/version/operation receipt under finite backoff, bounded attempts and durable next-due scheduling. An unresolved unknown stage becomes visible quarantined liability with an operator recovery path; never one-second polling or silent abandonment. A proposed capture-progress deadline is24hours; missing it quarantines and alerts, not permission to delete unverified recoverable data.

The archive lifetime is finite for ready archives. There cannot be a truthful finite deletion guarantee during an unresolved provider/storage outage: expose that failure, retain liability, close new admissions when safety envelopes fill and resume cleanup when service returns. Do not replace that reality with time-only accounting refunds.

## Global economic liabilities

One account's existing physical reservation continues to cover live/frozen/archived retained bytes until confirmed release. Count archives, service-owned pins, staged manifests/restores, unknown effects and cleanup metadata in account/fleet byte and row envelopes. Keep a finite global archive-operation count and concurrent worker/read/restore stock; due work uses durable indexes rather than fleet scans. Existing owner and essential floors must fund the largest **single bounded phase**, including metadata and external claims. Explicit cancellation/expiry must not be starved by optional compaction.

Before accepting paid/trial obligations, reserve the promised retention/headroom capacity. A full archive pool must stop new commitments or purchases, not delete existing promised archives early. Paid-to-free transitions remain allowed and consume retained liability; they are not forced through the new-free-account admission cap. Operator free/paid ratio controls consider actual occupied/retained liabilities and work, not a claim that logical virtual reservations are already a vendor storage charge.

## Paid capacity must be provisionable before checkout

Current policy `authorizeBillingPurchase` only checks account lifecycle restrictions. `accountCapacity` reports current entitlement, not physical max-plan provisioning. The Polar adapter checks those policy gates, but it does not reserve or prove that a10,000,000,000-byte plan fits the actor's fixed physical profile. Existing free deployments can use1–2GiB slices; an entitlement projection does not enlarge them. Storage/work profile digests and backend bindings deliberately reject casual configuration edits. This is a real paid-readiness gap.

The smallest honest first paid deployment option is **pre-size a fresh installation's uniform account slice for the largest offered plan**, plus native replacement/staging, metadata, log/export/archive/restore headroom, and enough fleet slices/rows for its admitted accounts. Unused physical allowance is an admission reservation, not actual stored bytes billed by R2. This reduces the number of accounts admitted under a chosen fleet risk envelope; tune free admission/paid-to-free safeguards honestly. Existing small-slice installations cannot upgrade by editing an environment variable; they need a separately implemented and validated migration or must keep purchases unavailable.

Add a trusted host port `reservePlanCapacity({subject,planId,offerRevision,intentId})` before creating any checkout/trial. Its durable receipt binds current installation/route/profile, required logical/physical/row limits and held fleet slot/headroom. A fresh pre-sized profile can implement it using existing whole-slice admission plus a current profile proof; an undersized or exhausted profile refuses before any payment-provider checkout call. It must account for an account with no storage actor yet, not assume one exists. Checkout intent retries reuse the receipt. Keep the hold through the checkout's bounded provider validity and reconcile a late/unknown completion before release; do not expire capacity while a valid checkout can still charge. A verified subscription consumes/converts the reservation idempotently. Capacity lost after an accepted external payment is an incident/refund/support reconciliation case, never “payment succeeded, writes will retry forever.”

Future dynamic expansion is a separate implementation: reserve the delta in the control fleet, prepare a signed migration revision, fence/drain old leases and inflight I/O, validate durable actor pools/profile high-watermarks, atomically accept the enlarged binding, then commit the control reservation. Preserve spent work/replay markers; rollback only after confirmed no effect. Never reset ledgers or shrink below retained/unknown/archived liability. No such migration is claimed here. A simple pre-sized profile is preferred until this path is implemented and independently qualified.

## Implementation sequence and required evidence

1. Reconcile funded warning semantics and persist exact cancellation intents/status reconciliation with no vendor activation; retain servicing when new sales are disabled.
2. Implement nonterminal account lifecycle acceptance, service-owned manifest references and current short-lived maintenance proofs; test Node disk and actual Worker SQL/R2, including restart/unknown outcomes.
3. Implement bounded archive/download/restore/expiry adapters and wire policy ports for the new profile. Preserve owner availability, session fences, shared dependencies and confirmed-deletion liability rules.
4. Add trusted paid capacity proof/reservation and deny checkout before vendor access when not provisionable. Test10GB offer against a2GiB profile refuses; a fresh pre-sized profile admits without changing anchored digests. No live purchase is required.
5. Qualify both abandonment and funding-lapse scenarios through actual maintained identity, multiple apps and native objects above4MiB; include expiry/download/restore races, user returning during cancellation/capture, new funding, changed subscription, revoked owner, offline UI recovery, stalled sink/storage/vendor results, fleet exhaustion and shared/service safeguards. Capture UI evidence before paid readiness.

Billing may remain disabled for initial free launch, but this work remains an explicit implementation obligation and paid activation gate, not a completed feature hidden by configuration.
